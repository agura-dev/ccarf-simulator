How the exam works

  • The scenario is always shown on the left: the context shared by several questions.
  • Each question has 4 options and a single correct answer.
  • The time remaining is shown at the top right. When it reaches 0 the exam is submitted automatically.
  • You can flag questions and, before finishing, see the status of all of them on the review screen.
  • Answer this practice question: it doesn't count toward your score.
